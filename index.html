Exercise 1 · Task placement
17 groups · 6 stakeholder perspectives

Where do we believe AI agents will play a role in the property journey?

Each group was assigned one stakeholder and placed 10 journey tasks on a scale from Manual to fully AI-Delegated. Below, one chart per stakeholder shows every group's placement side by side, sorted from "humans stay in the loop" at the top to "AI agents drive" at the bottom.

How to read the charts

Manual / Physical
Humans do this themselves.
AI-Assisted
AI executes user-prompted tasks.
AI-Represented
AI executes broader goals; human approves.
AI-Delegated
AI acts autonomously on your behalf.
Each row is one task. Each is one group, sitting in the zone where they placed that task. The vertical mark is the group average, so the further right it sits, the more the room expected AI agents to take the lead on that task. Tasks are sorted by that average: humans in the loop at the top, agent-driven at the bottom.
